01 Acceptance and scope

These terms apply to your access to the StackOps IT Solutions website and, where relevant, your use of services provided by StackOps. By accessing the website or using a service, you agree to follow these terms and any instructions that apply to that service.

Some services are arranged for a business or other organization. In that case, your organization may set additional requirements for its users, and a service proposal or other customer-specific arrangement may describe the service scope, configuration, and responsibilities in more detail. If you are using a service on behalf of an organization, you should be authorized to do so.

02 Services and customer responsibilities

StackOps provides IT and related services according to the service selected and the configuration agreed with the customer. Features, integrations, support arrangements, and deliverables can differ between services and customers.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information, maintaining any permissions and access needed for the service, choosing appropriate settings, and ensuring that their users are authorized to use it. Please review service outputs and use your own judgment before relying on them for important operational decisions.

03 Accounts and security

Where a service uses an account, each user should keep their sign-in details confidential, use the account only as authorized, and promptly tell the relevant organization or StackOps if they suspect unauthorized access. Do not share passwords or authentication secrets in support requests.

Customers should manage user access and permissions for systems they control, including connected third-party accounts. StackOps may ask for information reasonably needed to verify a support request or an instruction from an organization.

04 Acceptable use

Use the website and services lawfully and in a way that does not interfere with others or compromise systems. You must not attempt to gain access to systems or data without authorization, disrupt or overload a service, introduce malicious code, misuse another person's credentials, or use the services to infringe another person's rights.

If you identify a suspected security issue or misuse, contact support@stackopsit.co.za with relevant details. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or other secrets in your message.

05 Third-party services and integrations

Some services may connect with products or platforms provided by other organizations, such as Microsoft or WhatsApp. An integration is used only as enabled and authorized for the relevant service. Customers are responsible for reviewing their configuration and ensuring they have authority to connect the applicable accounts or data.

Third-party services are operated by their respective providers and may be subject to their own terms, privacy information, availability, and settings. StackOps does not control those independent services or their changes.

06 Availability and changes

We work to keep our website and services useful and available, but access may occasionally be interrupted for maintenance, updates, security measures, technical issues, or circumstances outside our control. Availability can also depend on customer systems and third-party providers.

We may update, improve, or change website content and service features over time. Where a change materially affects a customer service, we will handle communication in a manner appropriate to the service and the arrangements with that customer.

07 Service information and limitations

Website content and general service descriptions are provided to help explain what StackOps offers; they are not a substitute for advice tailored to your circumstances. We aim to describe services clearly, but specific features and outcomes depend on the selected service, configuration, customer environment, and any applicable service arrangements.

No description on this page should be read as a guarantee that a service will be uninterrupted or suitable for every purpose. If you need clarification about a specific service or its expected results, please discuss it with StackOps before relying on it.

08 Ending or restricting use

You may stop using the website at any time. For a customer service, contact your organization or StackOps to discuss how to end or change the arrangement, including any steps needed for accounts, connected systems, or customer data.

Access may need to be restricted or a service paused where there is a security concern, suspected misuse, an operational issue, or a change to the relevant customer arrangement. Where appropriate and practical, StackOps will communicate with the customer about the situation and next steps.

09 Governing details and contact

These general terms do not specify a governing law or replace any service-specific agreement made with a customer. If you have a question about which terms apply to a particular service, contact StackOps for clarification.

For questions about these terms or a StackOps service, email support@stackopsit.co.za.
