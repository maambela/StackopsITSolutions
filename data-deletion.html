Data Deletion Instructions

Use these instructions to ask StackOps to delete or de-identify data that we hold or process in connection with our website or services.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

01 Submit a deletion request

Email support@stackopsit.co.za with the exact subject line Data Deletion Request. This gives our team the information needed to review your request and respond.

Email: support@stackopsit.co.za
Subject: Data Deletion Request

02 What to include

To help us identify the relevant service and information, include:

  • The StackOps account, product, or service involved, if known.
  • The name of the requesting organization, if the request relates to an organization or its service.
  • Your contact email address and, if useful, your role or relationship to that organization.
  • Enough non-sensitive detail to help identify the data or account, such as a username, relevant date range, or record reference.
  • A short description of what you would like deleted and any specific system or integration involved.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, access tokens, recovery codes, or other secrets. We will contact you if additional information is needed.

03 Verification and authorization

Before acting, StackOps may need to verify your identity and confirm that you are authorized to make the request for the relevant account or organization. We may ask for reasonable additional information through an appropriate channel. We will not ask you to send your password or authentication secrets.

If we cannot confirm the request or its scope, we may need to ask for clarification before proceeding.

04 Organization-managed data

Some information is provided by or managed for a customer organization. If the data belongs to your employer, client, or another organization, contact that organization's administrator or privacy contact as well. The organization may need to confirm the request, decide what should be removed, or instruct StackOps as the service provider.

StackOps can review data within our control and the instructions applicable to the service, but we may not be able to remove information from systems managed directly by the organization or another provider.

05 Review and processing

We review each request in context, including the service involved, who controls the information, active service or contract needs, and applicable legal or record-retention obligations. These factors may affect whether information can be deleted immediately, what part of it is eligible for action, and whether we need instructions from the customer organization.

Where deletion is appropriate and within our control, eligible information will be deleted or de-identified where practical. Information that must be retained for an active service or other applicable obligation may be retained for as long as needed for that purpose. Backup copies may remain until they are removed through the normal backup lifecycle and are not generally available for routine use.

We do not promise a fixed completion period. The time needed depends on verification, the systems involved, and any required customer or organizational review.

06 WhatsApp and Meta data

These instructions cover data held or processed by StackOps. If your request concerns data on Meta or WhatsApp platforms, you may also need to submit a deletion or privacy request directly through the relevant Meta or WhatsApp account and privacy tools. StackOps cannot delete data from a platform account or system that Meta controls.

07 Confirmation and contact

We will respond to acknowledge or clarify the request and, after review, provide a status update or confirmation of the action taken where we can. If some information cannot be deleted, or if another organization needs to act, we will explain the next step where appropriate.

For a request or a question about this process, email support@stackopsit.co.za with the subject Data Deletion Request.
